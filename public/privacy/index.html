Who operates this program

The Wolcowitz Family Group Text is a private family SMS program operated by Jack Wolcowitz (“I,” “me,” or “we”) in New York. This policy explains how information is handled for that program.

Information we collect

We collect only your name and mobile phone number so we can deliver group messages to you and identify you to other members of the family group. We do not collect browsing data, payment information, or other personal data through this website for the SMS program.

How we use this information

Your name and mobile number are used only to operate the private family group text: adding you after you consent, delivering messages you and other members send, honoring opt-out requests, and providing help if you ask for it. This program is not used for marketing or selling.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are relayed only to the other members of this private family group so the conversation can continue. We may also use a telecommunications provider (currently Twilio) solely to send and receive the texts that make the group work.

How long we keep information

We keep your name and mobile number only for as long as you are a member of the group. If you leave or reply STOP, we remove you from the group and stop using your number to send group messages, except as needed to honor the opt-out and keep a record that you asked not to be contacted.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or about your information, email Jack Wolcowitz at jack11230@gmail.com.
